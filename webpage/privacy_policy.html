signavi - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2025-03-13

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the signavi mobile application ("the App") developed by 煮物とハンバーグ (Nimono to Hamburg) ("we," "us," or "our"). We provide this App as a free service and it is intended for use "as is."

2. Information Collection and Use

No Personal Data Collection: The App does not collect or store any personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, or location data).

Camera Usage: The App uses the device’s camera solely to detect road signs in real time. All processing occurs locally on your device, and no images or videos are uploaded to any server.

No Analytics: We do not integrate analytics services or advertising networks that gather user data. Thus, we do not track IP addresses, usage patterns, or location data.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services that collect or process user data. Therefore, no user information is shared or transmitted to any external parties.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it promptly.

5. Security

Since the App does not collect or transmit personal data, security risks are minimal. We do not store any user data on external servers.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here, and by continuing to use the App after such changes, you agree to the updated policy.
